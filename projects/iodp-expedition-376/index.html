2018 · JOIDES Resolution

IODP Expedition 376

In 2018 I spent two months at sea with the International Ocean Discovery Program as New Zealand's education and outreach officer aboard the JOIDES Resolution, as it retrieved rock core samples from the active undersea Brothers Volcano.

My role involved the creation of educational resources, video, 360° VR content, blogs, music, visual art and social media posts to promote the groundbreaking science of Expedition 376.
