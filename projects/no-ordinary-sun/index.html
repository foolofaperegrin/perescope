No Ordinary Sun

Unreal Engine 5.6 · GAME202

Never again shall the fading green of your magic emanations make pure again these polluted skies... for this is No Ordinary Sun.

Unreal 5.6 puzzle-story prototype with snowboard-esque movement mechanics, militaristic bipedal birds and ominous vibes.

This solo-developed 3D vertical game slice showcases my experience in Unreal Engine 5 — character creation, level design, state-machine-based animation, Blueprint coding, 3D modelling, geometry node systems, PBR texturing, world-space texturing, procedural content generation, kit-bashing and more.

No Ordinary Sun's title is a homage to a work of the same name, written by the late poet Hone Tuwhare (Ngāpuhi, 1922–2008). Tuwhare spent time deployed in Japan in the aftermath of World War II and witnessed first-hand the ruin of nuclear devastation. His renowned poem No Ordinary Sun is a rich and evocative lament regarding nuclear annihilation, resistance and futility. These are among the themes explored in this game prototype. Nei rā te mihi ki a koe e Hone, i ōu kupu whakamanawa.

No Ordinary Sun is a movement-based puzzle game prototype set in a bleak, dystopian world in crisis. The player — somewhere between a penguin, an albatross and a human — begins in a dark and noisy underground chamber, holding a strange glowing red orb. Thousands of their kind look down upon them before they are sent on their way to an overworld devoid of characters.

The environment is a rugged, barren volcanic slope covered in sand and clad in brutalist concrete structures. Heading downhill, the player enters an empty city blaring with sirens. A gate blocks their progress. The player must move about the level at pace using snowboard-esque mechanics and a unique teleportation method to open the gate and proceed to the great wall where they will undertake a most woeful deed.

Developed and completed for VUW's GAME202, the project was an exercise in rapid prototyping, asset pipelines, and level design using Unreal Engine 5.6.
