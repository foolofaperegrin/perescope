Grains

Synth player, song-writer and producer. Beginning in 2019 as an ambient synth project utilising Wellington-made synthesisers, Grains has undertaken a wide variety of composition work and specialty performances.

In 2020 we expanded to a five-piece band, gained a dedicated local following, and went on to perform at summer festivals including Eyegum's Welcome to Nowhere and Twisted Frequency. We released our debut self-titled album in April 2024.
