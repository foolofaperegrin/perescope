Oct 2024 · Wānaka Arts Society

Wānaka Arts Society — artist in residence

In October 2024 I spent two weeks on the shores of beautiful lake Wānaka as the Artist in Residence for the Wānaka Arts Society's 45th annual Labour Weekend Exhibition.

I stayed in a cosy cottage with a view of snowcapped mountains, and spent my days focusing on creating and developing my practice for the exhibition at the residency's conclusion.

More than 140 artists exhibited over the weekend, with more than 400 pieces of art.

Beneath my works, printed and mounted by PrintArt, I was thrilled to let the audience get hands-on with one of the microscopes and a large screen so they could explore the miniscule world around us.

During the residency, I ran some creative workshops at Mt Aspiring College, where art students used the microscope cameras to photograph their own subjects.
