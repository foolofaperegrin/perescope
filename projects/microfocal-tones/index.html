Microfocal Tones

Microscope-Driven Granualar Synthesiser · Max-MSP

Sobel-Kernel derived audio-visual synthesiser prototype built in Max-MSP

Video input is analysed for "focus". This value is used to drive paramaters of a granular synthesiser. In focus video produces brighter tones, while out of focus becomes washed and subdued. User can create presets and interpolate between these presets. Four channels for audio input samples, with controls exposed for granular synth inputs on those samples (grain size, scattering, envelope, etc.)

Made specifically for use with live microscope feeds, in which focus is constantly changing!
